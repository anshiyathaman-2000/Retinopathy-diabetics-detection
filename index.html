Model Architecture Specifications

Backbone Network: Pre-trained ResNet50. Frozen Conv1 through Layer2; Layer3 and Layer4 are fine-tuned.

1x1 Conv Projector: Projects 2048 ResNet features down to a 256-dimension representation.

Transformer Encoder: 2-Layer multi-head attention network modelling spatial correlation across 64 patch tokens.

Recurrent Sequence Model: Bidirectional LSTM layer for spatial sequence progression.

Classification Header: Linear layer mapped through Binary Cross-Entropy.

Illumination-Conscious Preprocessing Pipeline

Clinical eye fundus imaging frequently suffers from poor lighting, camera blur, and varying contrast. To normalize these inputs before feature learning, we implement an explicit two-stage image enhancement pipeline:

1. L-Channel CLAHE

Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization splits the image into the LAB color space (Lightness, A-color, B-color) and applies contrast optimization solely to the Lightness channel. This highlights critical diagnostic indicators (e.g. microaneurysms, exudates, and hemorrhage patches) without distorting natural hue details.

ClipLimit: 2.0 | TileGridSize: 8x8

2. Multi-Scale Retinex (MSR)

Retinex theory parses color images by extracting localized reflectance features from the illumination gradient. Our Multi-Scale Retinex averages logarithmic differences of Gaussian-blurred representations across multiple kernel widths, neutralizing shadows and lighting imbalances across the curved retina.

Scales: [5, 15, 30] | Weight: Average

RFMiD Challenge Dataset Context

The Retinal Fundus Multi-Disease Image Dataset (RFMiD) is annotated for a large number of diseases, making it a critical benchmark for multi-label deep learning. In this project, we evaluate on the validation subset consisting of 128 samples (split from the 640 challenge validation set), grading them against 45 categories and 1 global disease risk class.
